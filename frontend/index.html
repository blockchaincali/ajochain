AjoChain

Ajo / esusu savings, enforced by smart contracts on Portaldot

Portaldot

Save together. No collector needed.

  1. Create a group: contribution, number of members, round length.
  2. Join by locking one contribution as collateral. The group starts when it is full.
  3. Contribute each round. The full pot goes to that round's member automatically.
  4. Missed a round? After the deadline anyone can close the round: collateral covers the gap.
  5. Finish the cycle cleanly and earn a soulbound AjoBadge, your on-chain savings record.
